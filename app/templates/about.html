About Idea Tracker

Idea Tracker is a platform for collecting, discussing and evaluating innovative ideas. Anyone can browse; members submit, vote and comment; reviewers decide; administrators keep the platform healthy.

Roles

  • Submitter – submits ideas, votes once per idea, comments.
  • Reviewer – evaluates ideas and records a visible decision.
  • Administrator – manages users, categories and feedback.
