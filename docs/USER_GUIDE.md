# User Guide

## Getting started

1. Click **Sign up**, choose a username, e-mail and a password (8+ characters with letters and digits).
2. You are signed in automatically. Use **Sign in** next time with your username *or* e-mail.
3. The theme button (moon/sun) in the top bar switches dark mode; by default the app follows your system setting.

Anyone can **browse** ideas. You must be signed in to submit, vote and comment.

## Submitting an idea

1. Click **New idea**.
2. Enter a clear **title** and a **description**: the problem, your solution, the expected benefit.
3. Pick a **category** – or type a new one in "…or create a new category" (it will be added for everybody).
4. Click **Submit idea**. Your idea starts as **Pending**.

You can **edit or delete** your idea while it is *Pending*. After a reviewer has handled it, it is locked.
Find all of your ideas under **My ideas**.

## Finding ideas

On the **Ideas** page use the filter bar: free-text search, category, status and sorting
(*Newest, Trending, Top voted, Most discussed, Oldest*) and the status tabs. Click a member's name to see their public profile. The search box in the top bar searches titles and descriptions too.

## Voting

* ▲ = I like this idea, ▼ = I don't.
* You have one vote per idea. Click the same arrow again to take your vote back, or click the other arrow to change it.
* You cannot vote on your own idea.

## Commenting

On an idea page, write your comment under **Discussion**. Click **Reply** below any comment to answer it – replies
are shown indented beneath the original. You can delete your own comments (administrators can delete any).

## Idea statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | Waiting for a reviewer |
| Under review | A reviewer is evaluating it |
| Approved | Accepted for realisation |
| Rejected | Not pursued (the reviewer explains why) |
| Implemented | Done! |

## Reviewers

Open **Review** in the menu (the badge shows how many ideas are waiting). The queue lists ideas oldest-first with one-click *Start review*, *Approve* and *Reject*. For a written decision open the idea. If your account has the **reviewer** role, idea pages show an *Evaluation* panel in the right column: choose a status,
add a note visible to everybody, and save. You cannot evaluate your own ideas.

## Administrators

The **Admin** menu offers:
* **Dashboard** – counts and charts of ideas by status and category.
* **Users** – change roles (submitter / reviewer / administrator), disable or enable accounts.
* **Categories** – add, rename, delete (only when empty).
* **Feedback** – messages sent by users; mark them resolved.
* **Export** – download all ideas as CSV (opens in Excel).

## Feedback

Spotted a bug or have an improvement idea for the platform itself? Use **Feedback** in the menu or footer. Administrators read every message.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---|---|
| "Your form session expired" | Reload the page and submit again |
| I can't vote | Sign in first; you can't vote on your own idea |
| I can't edit my idea | It has already been reviewed – add a comment instead |
| Account disabled | Contact an administrator |
| "Too many failed attempts" | Wait 15 minutes before trying again |
